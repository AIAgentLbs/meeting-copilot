Choose between an open-source, locally stored recording workflow and Granola's managed AI notepad. Both offer bot-free capture. Keeping the original audio and inspecting the application source are the more useful dividing lines.

Choose MeetOnTrack if...

You want to retain the meeting audio, inspect or adapt the application, and check what is said against documents and Git history already on your computer. You are comfortable with a technical beta and managing the AI connection yourself.

Choose Granola if...

You want a managed notepad that improves your notes, searches meeting history and supports sharing across devices. You prefer the vendor to operate the service and do not need a retained audio recording in Granola.

A notepad is not an audio archive

Granola's no-stored-audio approach can be useful when your policy favors retaining text rather than recordings. MeetOnTrack makes a different choice: it keeps local media so you can return to the original. Neither choice is universally more private. Decide what must be retained, where it may be processed and who may receive it.

Granola capture, processing and audio retention

Try this before switching

If audio replay is essential, verify that you can keep and reopen the original recording. If it is prohibited, verify your retention policy before enabling local recording. Then test note quality on your own terminology rather than relying on a generic accuracy claim.

Compare the package you can actually install

The pinned packages are Mac v0.1.9 and Windows v0.1.4. They have different requirements and limitations. The source tree can be newer than either package. Development previews on the homepage do not extend the public beta's feature scope.

Automatic multilingual recognition, model choices, update notifications and newer integrations must be checked against the exact installed build. We do not claim tested parity between operating systems, guaranteed speaker attribution, fully offline AI or enterprise compliance certification.

How this comparison was made

Published by AI Agent Labs, maker of MeetOnTrack. This is a vendor-authored comparison, not an independent accuracy benchmark. Competitor capabilities come from the linked official product, help, pricing and policy pages, checked on 2026-10-16. We did not run comparative performance tests.

We use the same criteria for every product. An undocumented feature is marked unverified, not absent. Device capture, on-device speech recognition, cloud storage and cloud AI are separate questions. A free plan, public SDK or MCP endpoint is not an open-source licence for the whole application.

Pricing links point to current plans instead of mixing annual and monthly quote amounts. Plan limits and capture modes can change. Obtain the required participant consent regardless of whether a bot appears in the meeting.
