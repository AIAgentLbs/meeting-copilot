vs MeetScribe

Both advertise bot-free meeting capture. The important questions are which product you are installing, what source rights it provides, and where processing happens. MeetScribe, Meetily Community and Meetily PRO are not interchangeable products.

MeetScribe is based on Meetily, but it is not Meetily

MeetScribe credits Zackriya Solutions' Meeting Minutes as its foundation; that repository now redirects to Meetily. The upstream repository has an MIT licence and documents local Whisper/Parakeet transcription, Ollama summaries and optional external AI providers. It describes PRO as a separate codebase. Those upstream capabilities and source rights do not establish the capabilities or licence of the complete MeetScribe distribution. MeetScribe's third-party notices are attribution, not a published full-product source grant.

Try this before switching

Install the exact candidate on your own OS. Test a short multilingual call, replay and export, then inspect processing destinations with external AI disabled. Ask the vendor to resolve its processing and plan descriptions before using sensitive recordings. Do not infer offline behavior from an upstream project or a marketing illustration.

Compare the package you can actually install

The pinned packages are Mac v0.1.9 and Windows v0.1.4. They have different requirements and limitations. The source tree can be newer than either package. Development previews on the homepage do not extend the public beta's feature scope.

Automatic multilingual recognition, model choices, update notifications and newer integrations must be checked against the exact installed build. We do not claim tested parity between operating systems, guaranteed speaker attribution, fully offline AI or enterprise compliance certification.

How this comparison was made

Published by AI Agent Labs, maker of MeetOnTrack. This is a vendor-authored comparison, not an independent accuracy benchmark. Competitor capabilities come from the linked official product, help, pricing and policy pages, checked on 2026-10-19. We did not run comparative performance tests.

We use the same criteria for every product. An undocumented feature is marked unverified, not absent. Device capture, on-device speech recognition, cloud storage and cloud AI are separate questions. A free plan, public SDK or MCP endpoint is not an open-source licence for the whole application.

Pricing links point to current plans instead of mixing annual and monthly quote amounts. Plan limits and capture modes can change. Obtain the required participant consent regardless of whether a bot appears in the meeting.
