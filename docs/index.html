THE EVIDENCE LOOP

Not another meeting summary.

A local intelligence layer that connects the conversation to the work.

  1. 01

    Capture

    System audio and your microphone become a near-live transcript on your Mac. No bot joins the call.

  2. 02

    Resolve

    The current project, entities and speakers are identified from the meeting and your selected context.

  3. 03

    Verify

    Codex searches up to 15 local GitHub or GitLab checkouts, docs, source and Git history.

  4. 04

    Surface

    Only useful signals appear—with file, heading, symbol and commit provenance.

LOCAL BY DEFAULT

Your context stays yours.

The UI is available only at 127.0.0.1. Transcripts, screenshots, notes and reports stay in your user directory unless you explicitly configure delivery.

  • No repository upload
  • No background clone, pull or fetch
  • Read-only repository inspection
  • Optional Drive, email and Telegram delivery
