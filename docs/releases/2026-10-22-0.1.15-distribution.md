release. Owner approved publishing and the website update right after the
check on the owner's Mac ("да, публикуй сразу после проверки"). Same process
as [0.1.14](2026-10-21-0.1.14-distribution.md); this record lists what differs.

## Owner Mac update

Installed before publication from the exact archive (local file URL with its
SHA pin), after the call's transcript was finished and Capture had quit.
Version 0.1.15, numpy and Pillow import in the bundled Python, `config.json`
unchanged, Capture relaunched. Delivery shows no open issues, Check AI is
ready on every task, the speech check passes. UI: no language buttons in the
header, the removed helper lines are gone, the transcript can be hidden, and
the delivery pill reads plain "Delivery". `/api/updates` reports 0.1.15 with
no newer version.

## Explicit limits

No clean Mac and no audio hardware tests. Mac is ad-hoc signed and not
notarized; Windows is unsigned. No automatic installer is enabled. Never
restart an active recording to update.
